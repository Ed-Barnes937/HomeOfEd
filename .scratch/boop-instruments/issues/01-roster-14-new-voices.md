# 01 - The 20-instrument roster: 14 new voices, manifest, placeholder artwork

**What to build:** The launch kit grows from 6 to 20 instruments - pure kit
data, no engine code. `public/kits/launch/kit.json` gains the 14 new entries
(spec §2: Clap, Shaker, Cowbell, Woodblock, Triangle, Cymbal, Bass, Bell,
Chime, Pluck, Boing, Pop, Zap, Drip), keeping the classic six **first** in
manifest order (defaults and sample clips key off those positions). Roles:
the new drums `perc` (Clap and Shaker could argue for `snare`/`hat` but stay
`perc` - V1 ignores roles), the notes and silly group `melodic`.

Sounds are synthesized by extending
`apps/boop/scripts/generatePlaceholderSamples.mjs` in its own idiom, with new
building blocks where needed (vibrato for Boing, staggered noise bursts for
Clap). Per-voice character notes are in spec §2. Budget rules are unchanged:
peak 0.5 per voice, < 400 ms per file. The **Cymbal takes the full 400 ms as
a noise tail** - spec §10.3: noise tails add incoherently so it should pass
the 200 bpm retrigger rule; if the real test disagrees, trim the tail, never
touch `MAX_BPM`.

Artwork: game-icons.net CC BY 3.0 placeholders, per-icon authorship verified
against the upstream `game-icons/icons` source tree and recorded in
`public/kits/launch/ATTRIBUTION.txt`, exactly as ticket 18 did. Update the
sounds section of ATTRIBUTION with the 14 new voice descriptions too.

Spec: §2 (roster), §3 (sounds and artwork), §10.3 (cymbal).
