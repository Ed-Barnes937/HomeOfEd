# orbi — design brief

For: Claude Design. Full concept: [spec.md](spec.md).

## What orbi is

A planet tamagotchi for kids (designed with an 11-year-old). You build a crazy
planet from a set of dials, fast-forward time, and life emerges to suit
whatever world you made — climbing from bare rock to spreading colour, to
little moving blobs, to city lights on the night side and a tiny rocket
launch. A robot-satellite Mission Control character narrates, warns, and gives
opt-in hints from a side panel. Planets are saved to a searchable shelf and
can be exported as postcards.

## Screens to design

1. **Planet shelf** — save-game style list of planets, with search. The entry
   point.
2. **Builder** — blank-canvas dials (size, distance from sun, water,
   atmosphere, colours/patterns, moons, rings) + a randomise button, with the
   planet previewing live.
3. **Main view (zoomed out)** — the planet spinning in dark starry space;
   fast-forward control; the rescue toolkit (nudge orbit, thicken/thin
   atmosphere, send ice comet, plant a seed); Mission Control side panel.
4. **Zoomed in** — surface view where life visibly develops; Mission Control
   panel collapses out of the way here.
5. **Postcard** — an exported keepsake picture of a planet.

Also wanted: the **Mission Control character** itself — a small friendly robot
satellite. It gives the app a face (icon, postcards, empty states). It has no
name yet; a name may fall out of the design.

## Mood

- Dark, starry, fairly **simple** space background.
- **Realistic-but-a-bit-cartoony**: the planet is rounded and touchable,
  colours richer than real life — a beautiful model, not a photo.
- **Calm, slow, floaty** when zoomed out — nothing flashes or shouts.
- **Livelier, Spore-like** when zoomed in watching life grow.
- The **night side glows** — city lights twinkling there is the emotional
  payoff of the whole app.
- One anchor idea to consider: controls as a **spaceship control desk**.

## Explicit instruction

**Explore multiple distinct visual directions** — do not converge on one.
Interface design is deliberately open; the mood notes above are ingredients,
not a locked style. The 11-year-old co-designer will pick between directions.

## Constraints

- Browser app, laptop-first.
- Audience is kids (~11); text must be friendly and readable, science woven
  into Mission Control's voice, never worksheet-like.
- Sibling apps for family feel: boop, espy, boids, karesansui (homeofed.com).
