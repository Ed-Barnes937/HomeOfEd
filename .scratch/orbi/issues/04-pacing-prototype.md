**Type:** prototype
**Status:** open
**Blocked by:** 03

## Question

Prove the sim model's numbers before any real code. Build a throwaway headless
prototype (via `/prototype`) that runs the ticket 02/03 model tick-by-tick and
answers:

1. Does bare-rock-to-blobs take **5–10 minutes of fast-forwarding** (the
   spec's pacing guardrail) for a reasonable starter planet?
2. Do all four recipes actually reach chapter 4+ — is no recipe a dead end?
3. Does a no-recipe planet stay convincingly bare, and does one toolkit action
   (comet, nudge) plausibly rescue it?
4. Are the "one nudge from two recipes" planets reachable, not vanishingly
   rare?

Output: tuned constants (or a tuning table) + a verdict on whether the model
needs reshaping, linked from this ticket. The prototype is throwaway; the
numbers are the asset.
