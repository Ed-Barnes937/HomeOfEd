# 03 — Chapters, events and the rescue toolkit

**Type:** grilling
**Status:** open
**Blocked by:** 02

## Question

On top of the sim model from ticket 02, define progression and drama:

1. **The ladder**: what advances a planet through the five chapters (bare rock
   → seas → first life → blobs → clever blobs)? Per-chapter entry conditions,
   and whether chapters can regress (spec: lenient — life dies back, planets
   go sad, never game-over).
2. **Events**: freezing oceans, mega-storms, asteroid — how are they rolled
   (seeded random? telemetry-triggered?), how often, how are they surfaced to
   the player (Blip announces), and what is their effect window?
3. **Toolkit**: nudge orbit / thicken-thin atmosphere / ice comet / seed —
   each maps to a dial or state change; define magnitudes, cooldowns (if any),
   and how each interacts with recipes (a comet can push a planet *into* a
   recipe).
4. **Blip's fact feed**: what telemetry/state changes generate messages, and
   how the hint ladder (fact → nudge → answer) derives from the gap between
   current state and the nearest recipe.
