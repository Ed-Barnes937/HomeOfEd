# 03 — App name

Type: grilling
Status: resolved

## Question

What is the app called? Drives the subdomain (`<name>.homeofed.com`), package
name (`apps/<name>`), Fly app name, and the spec/feature slug. Ed is mulling;
style precedent is short and evocative (espy, Mosa, Karesansui). Starters
floated during charting: Silt, Grit, Terra. Resolve in conversation with Ed.

## Answer

**Silt.** Chosen by Ed (2026-08-02) over Grit, Terra, and Scree — fine sediment
carried and settled by water fits the sand/water/lava sim, and it sits well
beside espy and Mosa.

Derived identifiers:

- Subdomain: `silt.homeofed.com`
- Package: `apps/silt`
- Fly app: `hoe-silt` (matches the `hoe-<name>` convention in every fly.toml)
- Spec/feature slug: the effort directory stays `.scratch/sand-sim/`; the spec
  refers to the app as Silt.
