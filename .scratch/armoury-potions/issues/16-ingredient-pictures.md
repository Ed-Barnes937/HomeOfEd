## Scope

1. **`scripts/ingredient-thumbs.mjs`**: for each ingredient, open `/potions#w=` with a small
   round flask holding four pours of it, a cork and no label, `?freeze=1.2`, on the real GPU
   (the same launch flags and ANGLE Metal check as `gpu-shots.mjs`), screenshot the box stage,
   crop to the bottle with a little margin, and convert with `/opt/homebrew/bin/cwebp` (`-crop`,
   `-resize 192 192`, `-q 82`) to `public/thumbs/ingredient-<id>.webp`. Re-running it reproduces
   the files; its header says how to run it. Each file under 20 KB.
2. **The picker:** each jar shows its picture (`alt=""`, the button keeps its name "Pour in
   {Label}") at 56 px on the phone and 64 px on desk, its label beneath; the "In the bottle" rows
   keep the plain swatch (small) beside the name. A picture that fails to load falls back to the
   swatch.
3. **ADR 0112**: what Ed chose and why; how the pictures are made and re-made; sizes and budget.
