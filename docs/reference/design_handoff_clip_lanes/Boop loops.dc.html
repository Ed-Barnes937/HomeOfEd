Starts you on an empty grid
Drag to fillSquares paint the same way grid cells do — press and drag across a lane to lay a clip down four times in a row.
Repeats are freeNo repeat counter to learn. The same square twice means it plays twice.
The grid says whose it isThe well is tinted to the clip you're on and named above it, so switching lanes never feels like the grid changed by itself.
One dashed square aheadThe next free square on your lane is outlined, which is the whole invitation to build a longer song.
Loops — three directions
Same rules in all three: the 4 bars on the grid are always a clip, tapping another clip switches the grid straight to it, and there is no save step. What changes is how the chain of clips is drawn and how the next clip gets made. All three are live — paint the grid, switch clips, hit play.
1a Clip bar
A shelf of clips under the grid. The dashed + makes an empty clip and drops you straight onto a blank grid. Names are automatic; tap one to rename it.
