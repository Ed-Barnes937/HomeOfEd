Why 40px

A 52px step made the lane cells read as wide bars rather than notes; at 40px the tile is closer to square and the 6px gutters give the ladder air. The whole clip is narrower, so more of the 16 steps fit before the rail has to scroll.

The tap target

The tile is now 40 × 20, so the column still carries the hit: a tap anywhere in the 40px column lands on the nearest 24px band. Narrowing the step costs 12px of horizontal slop, which matters less than the vertical precision the bands already handle.

Still open

Note names. “Play any song” is easier with do re mi on the lane edge as in 5a, and a name is what the screen reader needs anyway — but it costs width in the rail and a reading decision (solfège, letters, or numbers) we have not made.
