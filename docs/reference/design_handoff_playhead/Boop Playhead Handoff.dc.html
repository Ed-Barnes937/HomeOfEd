1Controllable playhead — design handoff

Two screens share one clock: pressing Play or scrubbing on either moves both. Drag the strips to scrub — the song strip snaps to bars, the clip rail and WHOLE LOOP snap to steps. Demo song: 3 clips, 8 placed positions, 32 bars, 110 BPM.

1bConservative — a WHOLE SONG strip above the ruler, built from the loop map's tick language and aligned to the lane columns. Tap or drag it to jump; the ruler numerals become tap targets too. In the grid well the bar-numeral row grows a 16-step scrub rail, and the existing playhead column stays put at 45% when stopped.
