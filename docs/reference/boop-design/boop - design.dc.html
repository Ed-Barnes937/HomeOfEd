What I assumed, and why it looks like this
DARK STAGE, NOT A PAGE
You asked for boop to have its own identity, so it does not inherit the fridge's warm kitchen wall. The grid sits on a slate-teal stage instead. Three reasons: six saturated instrument colours read cleanly against dark and mud against warm paper; a playhead can be light-on-dark without ever needing a flash; and a dark field makes an empty cell look like a slot waiting to be filled rather than blank paper.
THE ARTWORK IS A PLACEHOLDER
I cannot draw the six characters. These are open-licensed line drawings from game-icons.net (CC BY 3.0) tinted to each row's colour — real objects, one family, no emoji. They work at row-label size. They do not survive to note-mark size, which is the brief's strongest labelling requirement, so on phone the note mark falls back to a plain coloured pebble. See 1e for what the real set needs.
SHARED WITH THE REST OF HOMEOFED
Back-arrow to the hub, lowercase wordmark, the 52px mobile strip, 9px control radius, 44px minimum tap target — all lifted from apps/fridge. Type is Chivo (heavy, cheeky, not babyish) with Chivo Mono for the one numeric readout. Every string on screen is listed in 1f for you to rewrite.

WHOLE LOOP
Designer's note. The loop map is always visible and always shows all 16 steps, so the playhead never disappears — it just moves to the map. A cyan edge-glow on the side the playhead is on tells a child which way to swipe back. Swipes snap to the 4-step groups, so you always land on a bar line, never half a bar.

BRIEF FOR THE ILLUSTRATOR — THE REAL BLOCKER
The brief asks for one artwork asset that is both the row label and the note mark. Line art cannot do both: at 18px inside a phone cell these drawings turn to mush, which is why the phone note mark above is a bare pebble.

What the real set needs, per character: a detailed form for 40–64px row labels, and a single-silhouette form — one closed shape, no interior lines, recognisable at 18px — for the note mark. Same character, two crops. Six characters, shared DNA, distinguishable by outline alone.

Until then these placeholders are game-icons.net, CC BY 3.0 — attribution owed to Delapouite, Lorc, Caro Asercion and Skoll if any of it ships.
